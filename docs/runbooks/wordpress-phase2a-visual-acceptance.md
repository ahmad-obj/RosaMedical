Use this runbook against the disposable local WordPress foundation only. Do not use Hostinger or production as a substitute.

## Required routes

Review both:
- `/products/`
- the seeded **Stevens Scissors — Regular** Product Detail containing exact SKUs `04-0901` and `04-0911`.

## Cross-viewport checks

For every viewport:
- no horizontal document overflow;
- no clipped title, SKU, configuration, CTA or catalogue link;
- product media uses contained presentation and long instruments are not cropped to fill;
- card title/reference remain readable at the actual column count;
- only one page-level `<main>`;
- no cart, checkout, shipping, rating, sale or related-product language;
- Product Detail shows `Price on request` when no pricing provider is active;
- Stevens detail exposes exactly the seeded real configurations, including `04-0901` Straight and `04-0911` Curved.

## Keyboard and drawer acceptance

At a drawer-mode viewport:
1. Tab to the Menu button and open it with keyboard.
2. Confirm focus moves into the drawer.
3. Tab and Shift+Tab cycle within enabled drawer controls, including future inputs/selects if a language control supplies them.
4. Confirm main content and footer are inert while the drawer is open.
5. Press Escape; drawer closes and focus returns to Menu.
6. Reopen and activate a drawer navigation link; navigation begins without forcing focus back to the trigger first.
7. Confirm background scrolling is locked only while open.

## Accessibility and preference checks

- At 200% browser zoom, content reflows without horizontal page scrolling or hidden controls.
- Apply text-spacing overrides (line-height 1.5; paragraph spacing 2em; letter spacing .12em; word spacing .16em) and confirm no clipping/overlap.
- Enable `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`; non-essential card/media/shell motion disappears without losing state feedback.
- Check visible keyboard focus on header links, menu controls, product-card links, configuration radios, PDF links and quote/inquiry actions.
- Practical targets are at least 44px; primary controls aim for 48px.
- Selected configuration is communicated by native radio state + structural emphasis, not color alone.

## RTL structural check

Temporarily render representative shell/archive/detail markup under `dir="rtl"` and confirm:
- drawer enters from logical inline end;
- spacing/padding/insets remain correct;
- card action arrow mirrors;
- selected configuration edge emphasis mirrors;
- sticky action remains aligned and safe;
- DOM reading order is unchanged and sensible.
