## Art-direction review of the whole set

The five family covers supply useful color accents and are better kept as graphic navigation art. The `v5` instrument-focused photography gives Rosa a credible visual spine. The weak parts are the clinical handoff/operating-room scenes (IMG-006/007/016), the almost abstract soft-focus ring image (IMG-005/021), and excessive reuse of one dark instrument-tray photo (IMG-020). Replacing those with distinct, clear instrument or catalogue-reference imagery will add variety without turning the site into pure grayscale or generic hospital stock. No product thumbnail or SKU photo is part of this correction pass.

### Further mobile frame coverage correction

The owner identified the same top/bottom gaps in other mobile editorial images. Existing 390 px DOM evidence shows several image boxes retaining source-driven dimensions despite taller responsive frames: Home feature and evidence images rendered 390×260, Home support and Shop support rendered 350×233, and the About proof image rendered 390×524. Their parent sections and media slots have independent responsive heights. The common cause is a centered grid media slot with an image whose computed height can follow its source aspect ratio.

The editorial slot rule now positions the image inside its existing frame at `width: 100%`, `height: 100% !important`, and `object-fit: cover`. It targets the Home feature, support and evidence media, About feature/evidence banners, and Shop support image. The About/Contact page-hero image receives the same explicit fill. Client-selected WordPress images use the same fill treatment where those templates render a direct `<img>`. Logo, family catalogue covers and individual product media retain their separate sizing behavior. The CSS and focused PHP contracts pass static checks, but rendered verification of this further correction is pending browser access after the automatic usage-limit rejection.
