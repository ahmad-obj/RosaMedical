## Rights Position

All current production candidates are original editorial compositions made from the five client-supplied Rosa catalogue PDFs. No Getty, iStock, paid-stock, restricted editorial image or unauthorized web download is included.

The files are treated as client-controlled brand source material for review. Before public production publication, Rosa/the client must confirm that it owns or is authorized to reuse the photography and document artwork contained in the supplied catalogues. This is a legal sign-off requirement, not an identified infringement.

## External Web Research

Free-commercial-use searches were performed on Pexels, Pixabay and Wikimedia Commons. Candidates that were visually generic, clinical, lower resolution, historically mismatched or otherwise weaker than the Rosa-owned source system were excluded or retained as reference-only in `rejection-log.md`.
