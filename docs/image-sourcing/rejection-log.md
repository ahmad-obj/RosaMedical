# Rosa Medical Editorial Imagery Rejection Log

**Branch:** `preview/non-product-imagery-01`  
**Purpose:** preserve why serious candidates were excluded and prevent weak candidates from re-entering later review.

## Fixed Rejection Categories

- Rights unclear or redistribution prohibited
- Paid-only source
- Visible third-party branding
- Unsupported factory/certification/clinical implication
- Graphic clinical context
- Generic hospital or clipboard stock aesthetic
- Distorted or implausible instrument geometry
- Embedded text conflicts with UI
- Insufficient resolution for intended crop
- Weak desktop/mobile crop tolerance
- Visually inconsistent with selected Rosa set
- Product-specific image owned by the separate product-media lane

## Notes

- A free licence does not guarantee visual suitability.
- A candidate remains excluded until the rejection reason is deliberately resolved and documented.
- Reference-only imagery must never be copied into production asset directories or the final handoff ZIP.
